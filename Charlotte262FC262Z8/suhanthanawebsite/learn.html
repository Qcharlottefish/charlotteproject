What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Features of AI

  • Learning from information
  • Recognising patterns
  • Solving problems
  • Supporting decision-making

Advantages of AI

  1. Can improve productivity
  2. Can save time
  3. Can help analyse information
  4. Can support employees in their work

Limitations of AI

  • AI can make mistakes.
  • AI needs accurate information.
  • Some AI systems can be expensive.
  • Human judgement is still important.

AI in the Workplace

Work Area Example of AI Use
Customer Service Chatbots can answer customer questions.
Business AI can help analyse business data.
Education AI tools can support learning and research.
