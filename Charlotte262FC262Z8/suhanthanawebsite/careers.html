1. AI Engineer

AI engineers develop and maintain AI systems and applications. They use programming, data and machine learning knowledge.

Useful Learning Path

  • Learn programming basics
  • Learn machine learning
  • Practise building AI projects

Relevant Training: Microsoft Azure AI training

2. Data Scientist

Data scientists analyse data and use technology to find useful patterns and information.

Useful Learning Path

  • Learn statistics
  • Learn data analysis
  • Learn machine learning

Relevant Training: IBM Data Science training

3. AI Product Manager

AI product managers help organisations plan and develop products that use artificial intelligence.

Useful Learning Path

  • Learn AI fundamentals
  • Develop project management skills
  • Understand business and technology

Relevant Training: AI and project management courses

Why AI Skills Matter

AI skills can help students understand new technologies and prepare for digital workplace opportunities.

Continuous learning and professional training can help workers develop their knowledge throughout their careers.
